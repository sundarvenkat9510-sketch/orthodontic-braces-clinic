Clinical Services

Orthodontic Treatments & Solutions

Custom-tailored alignment therapies engineered for oral health, functional harmony, and beautiful facial balance. Every smile plan is uniquely yours.

Fixed Appliance 14 - 24 Mos

Traditional Metal Braces

The gold standard for reliable, complex orthodontic corrections. Constructed from surgical-grade stainless steel with contoured, low-friction brackets for improved oral comfort.

Key Advantages:
  • Unmatched three-dimensional root control
  • Ideal for severe crowding, impactions, and deep bites
  • Customizable colored elastics for children & teens
Tooth-Colored 14 - 22 Mos

Ceramic / Clear Braces

Translucent polycrystalline ceramic brackets specifically crafted to match your natural tooth shade. You get the full movement speed of fixed braces while preserving aesthetic confidence.

Key Advantages:
  • Blends seamlessly with natural tooth enamel
  • High-density ceramic resists staining & discoloration
  • Rounded bevel contours minimize mucosal irritation
100% Removable 6 - 18 Mos

Custom Clear Aligners

Custom thermoformed medical-grade polyurethane trays engineered using 3D digital dental scans. Each sequential tray applies calibrated micro-forces to guide teeth into perfection.

Key Advantages:
  • Completely removable for dining, brushing & flossing
  • Virtually unnoticeable in everyday social and work settings
  • No emergency wire pokes or bracket repairs
Concealed Behind Teeth 16 - 26 Mos

Lingual (Hidden) Braces

Fixed brackets custom-cast to conform precisely to the inside surfaces of your teeth. Completely invisible from the front, providing maximum cosmetic discretion with 24/7 fixed compliance.

Key Advantages:
  • Zero visibility to friends, cameras, or colleagues
  • 100% custom CAD/CAM gold alloy brackets
  • Ideal for actors, public speakers, and executives
Retention & Stability Lifetime Smile

Retainers & Post-Care

Protect your smile investment for decades to come. We provide clear Essix retainers, durable Hawley retainers, and bonded lingual wires backed by our digital 3D backup guarantee.

Key Advantages:
  • Prevents biological relapse and unwanted shifting
  • Digital cloud file storage for fast 1-day replacements
  • Includes ultrasonic hygienic cleaning case kit
Dentofacial Correction 12 - 24 Mos

Bite Alignment & Smile Correction

Orthodontics is about more than just straight front teeth. We correct overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites to prevent premature tooth wear and relieve TMJ jaw joint pain.

Key Advantages:
  • Corrects chewing efficiency and protects enamel
  • Relieves muscular jaw tension and TMJ soreness
  • Enhances facial symmetry and chin harmony
