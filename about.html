Mission & Vision

Crafting Confident Smiles with Clinical Precision

At AuraSmile Orthodontic Clinic, we believe every patient deserves a smile that radiates confidence and promotes lasting oral health. Established in 2010 by Dr. Sarah Jenkins, our clinic was conceived as an antidote to cold, clinical, high-stress dental offices.

We have designed our entire space—from ambient acoustic soundproofing and warm lighting to high-resolution digital touchscreens—to feel inviting, calm, and reassuring for adults, teenagers, and young children alike.

Our Vision

To be the benchmark in digital orthodontic care, where technological innovation and warm empathy intersect seamlessly.

Our Mission

To deliver pain-minimized, customized teeth alignment and bite corrections with transparent, accessible financial installments.

The AuraSmile Journey

Your 4-Step Patient Pathway

From your initial consultation to the day you reveal your transformed smile, our process is clear, transparent, and collaborative.

01
Comprehensive 3D Scan

We take high-definition 3D intraoral impressions and low-dose digital x-rays with zero gagging or putty.

02
Customized Plan & 3D Preview

Your orthodontist designs a computer-simulated model showing exactly how each tooth shifts over time.

03
Gentle Treatment Delivery

Whether you choose clear aligners or ceramic braces, appointments are gentle, punctual, and easy.

04
Lifetime Retainer Care

Custom retainers and annual checkups ensure your new aligned bite stays permanent for decades to come.

State-of-the-Art Equipment

Modern Dental & Orthodontic Technology

Investing in cutting-edge diagnostic and therapeutic technology ensures faster treatment times, heightened safety, and superior clinical precision.

iTero Element 5D Scanner

Captures 6,000 frames per second to render an ultra-precise 3D digital model of your teeth in under 3 minutes.

Ultra-Low-Dose CBCT Imaging

Green-technology cone beam CT and cephalometric imaging delivering up to 80% lower radiation than older film x-rays.

ClinCheck AI Simulation

Algorithmic tooth staging calculates optimal velocity, rotation angles, and staging for tooth roots and crowns.

Class-B Vacuum Sterilization

Hospital-grade steam autoclaves with biological spore testing and sealed packaging for every instrument.
