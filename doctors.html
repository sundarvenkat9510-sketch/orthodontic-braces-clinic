Our Patient Promise

A Doctor-Led, Patient-Centered Philosophy

At AuraSmile, we believe that orthodontics is both a fine art and a rigorous science. Unlike direct-to-consumer mail-order aligners, your smile journey is supervised at every single step by licensed orthodontic specialists.

One-on-One Orthodontist Care

You consult with the same lead orthodontist at each adjustment visit.

Conservative Treatment

We prioritize non-extraction and airway-friendly mechanics whenever feasible.

On-Time Appointments

We respect your schedule with low waiting room times and swift tray check-ins.

Zero Surprise Charges

All retainers, broken bracket repairs, and initial scans are clearly itemized upfront.
