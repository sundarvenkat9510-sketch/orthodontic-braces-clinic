Patient Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about orthodontic consultations, braces, aligners, and payment options.

Absolutely not! More than 35% of our orthodontic patients are adults. As long as your gums and supporting bone are healthy, teeth can be safely and effectively moved at any age. We offer discreet aesthetic options such as ceramic braces and clear aligners so you can straighten your teeth without feeling self-conscious.

Treatment duration varies depending on your specific malocclusion, biological response, and appliance compliance. Minor spacing or mild crowding with clear aligners can take as little as 6 to 9 months, while comprehensive bite corrections typically range from 14 to 22 months. During your initial consultation, we provide an exact estimated timeline based on your 3D scan.

Modern orthodontics is far gentler than in the past. You will experience mild tenderness or tightness for 2 to 3 days after getting braces fitted or when switching to a new aligner tray as your teeth begin shifting. This is normal and can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers and orthodontic wax.

We believe cost should never prevent someone from achieving a healthy smile. We offer transparent all-inclusive fee structures with zero surprise charges. We provide customized 0% interest monthly installment plans, flexible down payments, and assistance with dental insurance benefits. You can track all scheduled payments through our Patient Portal.

Teeth have a natural physiological memory and tendency to shift back (relapse) as bone remodels around the new positions. Retainers hold your teeth stable during this critical phase and preserve your smile for life. We offer durable clear Essix retainers, traditional Hawley retainers, and bonded lingual retention wires.
